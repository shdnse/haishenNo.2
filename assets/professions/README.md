# Independent profession reveal

Source: the user-provided [shared image conversation](https://chatgpt.com/share/6aa619b4-d1a8-83e8-ac2f-6fce344c01e4).

The first generation contains a matching pair: the complete four-character
artwork and its black-silhouette version. Both original PNG files are preserved
byte-for-byte here (1672 × 941); the later half-silhouette layout mockup is not
used as an interactive state.

Four separate SVG clipping masks and hit regions isolate the knight, prince,
witch and archer. Each foreground independently fades in and scales from 92.5%
to 100% around its feet. A static SVG luminance matte derived from the supplied
silhouette keeps the background outside the character from brightening. The entire scene is never enlarged or brightened on
hover. Clicking toggles each character's selection independently. Native buttons
provide keyboard and touch equivalents, with a polite selection announcement.

On narrow screens the scene remains large enough to see the artwork. Visitors
can swipe horizontally or tap a named profession to center it. Reduced motion
and the existing global motion control make the reveal immediate. Foreground
images share one decoded source and load only near the viewport. No game files,
audio, certificate assets, original wallpaper, or personal data are modified.
